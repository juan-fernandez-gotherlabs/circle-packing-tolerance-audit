`data/provenance.json` contains one entry for every candidate in the frozen
audit, plus the unranked Numaro claim.  It records the exact upstream URL or
identifier, the license status checked on 2026-08-08, and the redistribution
decision.

vendor the third-party program or coordinate file.  It preserves only derived
facts such as recomputed scores, feasibility margins, source identifiers, and
cryptographic audit output.
